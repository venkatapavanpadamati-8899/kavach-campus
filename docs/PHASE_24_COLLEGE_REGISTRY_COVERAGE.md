## 1. Executive Summary

As required by Phase 24D, the college registry was systematically audited and expanded beyond the initial pilot seed to provide authentic coverage across all **26 gazetted districts of Andhra Pradesh** (reorganized April 4, 2022).

The directory includes:
- **84 Verified Andhra Pradesh Institutions**
- **7 Premier National Engineering Colleges** (IIT Madras, IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, IIT Tirupati, NIT Warangal, NIT Calicut, BITS Pilani)
- **Total Master Registry:** **91 Institutions**
- **District Coverage:** **100% of Andhra Pradesh (26/26 Districts)**

Data sources utilized: **AICTE Official College Directory**, **APSCHE**, **SBTET AP**, **APCCE (Collegiate Education)**, **UGC Autonomous Register**, and **AISHE**.

## 4. Metadata Completeness & Verification

Each college entry in the registry contains complete operational parameters:
- **Unique Identifier (`id`)**: Lowercase slug format.
- **Official AISHE & AICTE Codes**: Standard educational identifiers for regulatory compliance.
- **GPS Coordinates (`coordinates`)**: Latitude and longitude down to 4 decimal places.
- **Geofence Radius (`geofenceRadiusMeters`)**: Calibrated perimeter (500m–1500m) for campus boundary detection.
- **Emergency Helplines**: Security control room, Women's cell, ambulance contact.
- **Campus Buildings Array**: Explicitly mapped building zones for incident precision routing.
- **Verification Status**: 100% synchronized with local PostgreSQL database table `public.colleges`.
